# Building your section (no coding needed)

You'll build your part of the site in free Claude (claude.ai), then send it to [lead] or [co-integrator], who will add it to the site.

## 1. Start a new chat in Claude and paste this
> Build one section of a website as a single HTML snippet with its CSS in a `<style>` tag. Make it an artifact.
> Section: **[your section: hero / about / features / contact]**
> Colors: background #ffffff, text #1d1d1f, main #2f5bea, accent #f2a541 [lead fills in final colors]
> Font: [font name]
> Rules: no header, footer or navigation menu. No JavaScript. No external libraries. Must look good on a phone.
> Content: [what your section should say or show]
> Before you build, ask me up to 3 questions and list anything you're assuming.

## 2. Keep asking for changes until you like it
For example: "make the title bigger" or "add three cards with icons."
Before sending, check your section against its "Definition of Done" in the plan ([lead] will share it).

## 3. Send it in
Copy the artifact's code (or its share link) and send it to [integrator] in [group chat]. Include:
- your section name
- any images (send the actual files, and say where they came from)

## Please don't
- change the colors or fonts. Ask [lead] if you want different ones.
- build a whole page. Just your section.
